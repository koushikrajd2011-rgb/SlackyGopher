# SlackyGopher - 
## A Slack Bot on Slack who loves Gophers and is one

SlackyGopher works in slack, just use the slash command /goship-slacky and it has variety of commands for you to try.

The available commands for now - 
* /goship-slacky , Say hello to slackygopher
* /goship-slacky-ping , Ping slackygopher to see if it works
* /goship-slacky-book , get a suggestion and read a new book!
* /goship-slacky-gopher , get a picture of a gopher plushieee
* /goship-slacky-country , get a country's breif details
* /goship-slacky-art , Find an Art along with it's detailss
* /goship-slacky-word , learn a new word from a gopher
* /goship-slacky-quote , Listen to one of slackygopher's fav quotess!
